About this experiment

Spherical rigid bodies, Earth gravity and a sealed cylindrical vial. The vial follows a prescribed sinusoidal stroke; no upward drift is imposed. Air drag, cohesion and irregular grain shapes are omitted.

The cutaway removes front small grains visually; the intruder stays whole. All grains remain in the physics. This is an illustrative PhysX model, not calibrated Hertz–Mindlin DEM or a validated reproduction of the published experiments. Rise magnitudes remain sensitive to numerical settings.
